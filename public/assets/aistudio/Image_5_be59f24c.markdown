## Brand & Style

This design system creates an archival, museum-grade editorial gallery tailored for high-end photography in South India. It celebrates heritage through contemporary minimalism, balancing cinematic depth, temple-architecture proportion, and modern editorial discipline.

### Personality & Audience
- **Brand Personality**: Timeless, aristocratic, understated, contemplative, and culturally grounded.
- **Target Audience**: Discerning couples, patrons of culture, creative directors, and high-net-worth clients seeking heritage weddings, artisanal portraits, and documentary-grade storytelling.
- **Emotional Response**: Quiet reverence, cinematic gravitas, tactile warmth, and permanence.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial High-Minimalism with Architectural Restraint**: Ample breathing room, hairline separation strokes, asymmetric spatial pacing, and large typographic statements paired with precise metadata typography.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from temple stone, bronze idols, and heirloom silk zari under low ambient light. It rejects harsh starkness in favor of warm, saturated graphite tones that let photography take precedence.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#121212`)**: Deep graphite charcoal. Avoid pure pitch `#000000` to prevent visual clipping of photograph shadows.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#181716`)**: Warm soot for cards, image backing frames, and hovering trays.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#22201D`)**: Soft obsidian for flyouts, dialogs, and elevated controls.
- **Primary Accent (`#C5A880`)**: Warm champagne bronze. Used for key interactive highlights, focal typography, and active indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (`#D4AF37`)**: Temple gold. Reserved exclusively for rare high-value actions, award laurels, and curated series badges.
- **Muted Neutral (`#7A7570`)**: Warm slate grey for caption labels, metadata, timeline marks, and hairline borders.
- **Curated Off-White (`#FBF9F5`)**: Creamy pearl for high-readability body copy, masthead titles, and display elements.
- **Subtle Ivory (`#F3EFEA`)**: Secondary text color with lower luminance to establish effortless hierarchy.

### Application Rules
1. Maintain dark mode as the default experience to emulate a private gallery viewing room.
2. Hairlines and dividers must use `#C5A880` at 18% opacity or `#7A7570` at 25% opacity; never use solid opaque borders.
3. The bronze accents must be applied sparingly; no more than 5% of the viewport should carry `#C5A880` or `#D4AF37`.

## Typography

The typographical rhythm relies on the tension between a high-contrast literary serif (`Playfair Display`) and a refined, geometric grotesque sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs generous vertical spacing and delicate italics for project locations (e.g., *Dindigul*, *Madurai*, *Chettinad*). Headings must remain light to medium in weight; never bold.
- **Body & Textual Passages (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Light weight (`300`) delivers clarity without drawing focus away from full-bleed photography.
- **Labels & Archival Metadata (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Capitalized with wide letter spacing (`0.18em`), mimicking catalog and museum specimen notation (e.g., `SERIES NO. 04 / 35MM`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around deliberate pacing, using asymmetrical pauses and generous whitespace to replicate an exhibition catalog.

### Grid Philosophy & Breakpoints
- **12-Column Fluid Grid**: Content flows dynamically across 12 columns with wide outer margins that frame the photography like gallery mat boards.
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: Columns use `gutter-desktop` (2.5rem) and `margin-desktop` (4.5rem). Editorial essays and side-by-side photo pairings use unbalanced column distributions (e.g., 7 columns for imagery, 4 columns for story, 1 column empty space).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: Columns use `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin-tablet` (2.5rem). Double-image diptychs remain intact; text stacks below the imagery.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `margin` (1.25rem). Photo series collapse into full-width vertical progressions with single-column metadata.

### Exhibition Framing
Images must never touch one another directly. Maintain a minimum gap of `space-md` or frame them inside archival passe-partout containers with `space-lg` internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is achieved through tone and surface temperature rather than heavy shadows.

### Atmospheric Depth Tiers
- **Tier 0 (The Void - `#121212`)**: The canvas root. Absorbs all extraneous detail and sets the viewing context.
- **Tier 1 (Framed Mats - `#181716`)**: Matte finish containers for photo carousels, diptych backdrops, and index items.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Overlays - `#22201D` at 85% opacity with 20px backdrop blur)**: Navigation bars, modal inspect sheets, and floating image control bars.

### Outlines & Ambient Diffusion
- **Hairline Framing**: Flat, 1px low-contrast borders (`#C5A880` at 16% opacity) trace containers and image frames. Never use hard, thick borders.
- **Ambient Glow Shadow**: For floating controls and lightbox images, use a subtle bronze-tinted blur:
  `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(18, 16, 14, 0.75), 0 0 20px 0 rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.04);`

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural sharpness with `roundedness: 0`.

### Angular Discipline
- **Zero Radius (`0px`)**: Cards, picture frames, buttons, drop-down menus, inputs, and chip filters are crisp and rectilinear. This reinforces the photographic film-strip format and gallery framing traditions.
- **Circular Exceptions**: Exclusively reserved for floating pagination pips, cursor rings, and audio play/pause toggles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Crisp rectangular container with a hairline border (`#C5A880`), dark soot background (`#181716`), and text in `label-caps` (`#FBF9F5`). On hover: background shifts smoothly to `#C5A880`, text color transitions to `#121212`.
- **Text Link Button**: Plain serif italic text followed by a thin, extended horizontal rule (`→`), animated through an underline draw-in effect.
- **Icon Action**: Square boundary (44px × 44px) with subtle 1px border (`rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.2)`), containing minimalist hairline vector icons.

### Gallery Cards & Exhibition Mats
- **Framed Picture Card**: Wrapped in a container with a 1px border (`#7A7570` at 20% opacity), with `space-sm` padding creating a thin matte edge around the photograph.
- **Metadata Tag**: Positioned directly beneath images; includes shooting location, camera format (e.g., `MEDIUM FORMAT / DINDIGUL`), and sequence index using `label-meta` in `#7A7570`.

### Chips & Series Filters
- **Filter Chip**: Flat, borderless text button using `label-caps`. Inactive state sits at `#7A7570`. Active state changes to `#C5A880` accompanied by an italic count mark (e.g., *08*).

### Form Fields & Inquiries
- **Input Fields**: Single bottom-border hairline only (`#7A7570` at 40% opacity). Transparent background. Placeholder text set in `body-md` in `#7A7570`. On focus: bottom border transitions to solid `#C5A880`, with zero drop-shadow glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px square with 1px border. Checked state displays a solid inner square inset of 3px in `#C5A880`.

### Navigation Masthead
- **Editorial Masthead**: Centered typographic monogram or wordmark in `Playfair Display`. Left and right wings contain widely spaced uppercase navigation items in `label-caps`. 
- **Sticky State**: Fixed at top using Tier 2 glass surface (`#121212` at 88% opacity with 16px blur) and a 1px bottom border in `rgba(197, 168, 128, 0.12)`.

### Specialized Exhibition Lightbox
- **Viewport Canvas**: Deepest dark (`#0A0A0A` at 96% opacity) with centered image constrained to 85vh. Side panels display aperture, focal length, film stock emulation, and historical context about the South Indian location.
